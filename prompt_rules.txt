CRITICAL UNITS OF MEASUREMENT RULE:

- FOR EMPTY WAGON RETURNS (SPS 0.10 CHF/axle-km), CAR TRANSPORTERS (Table 5 col 6), OR FIXED PER-WAGON RATES: Output rates strictly PER 1 WAGON (USD/wagon)!
